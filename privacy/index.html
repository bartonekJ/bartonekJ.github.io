Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 August 2026 · Last updated: 14 August 2026

JB_Drill is designed as a local-first application. It does not require a byBartonek account and the current Android and Windows builds do not include advertising, analytics or behavioral tracking.

1. Who operates JB_Drill

JB_Drill is provided by Jiří Bartoněk under the byBartonek name, Czech business identification number (IČO) 07924381. Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@bybartonek.com.

2. Data processed by the application

JB_Drill processes the content you create only to provide its editing, animation, library and export functions. This can include:

  • drill drawings, player labels, notes, tags and equipment layouts;
  • animation timing, tactical keyframes and application preferences;
  • file names and metadata needed to display the Drill Library;
  • PDF and video files you choose to export.

This content remains on your device or in a storage folder you explicitly choose. It is not uploaded to a byBartonek server.

3. Storage folder access

You may give JB_Drill access to a local folder or to a folder exposed by a third-party storage provider such as Google Drive. The application uses this access to read, create and update drill files and exports in that selected location. You can revoke access by changing the storage folder, clearing the app's data, uninstalling the app, or removing the permission in your operating system.

If you choose cloud-backed storage, the storage provider and operating system may transfer and synchronize the files under their own terms and privacy policy. byBartonek does not operate or control that synchronization service.

4. Local application data

JB_Drill stores limited settings on the device, such as the selected folder reference, color palette, tool state, copied drill data and interface preferences. These settings are used only to restore the application workflow. They can be removed by clearing the application's local data or uninstalling the application.

5. Network use and third parties

The current JB_Drill application does not send drill content, device identifiers or usage statistics to byBartonek. Sharing, cloud synchronization and opening exported files are handled by services or applications selected by you. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

6. Support communication

If you contact support, byBartonek processes the information you voluntarily provide — such as your email address, message, device details and attached example files — in order to respond and diagnose the issue. Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, legal or product-improvement records.

7. Why support data is processed

When you contact byBartonek, personal data in the message is processed to answer your request, provide support, take steps connected with a requested product or service, protect the application and users, and improve JB_Drill. Depending on the request, the legal basis is performance of a contract or steps requested before entering into one, byBartonek's legitimate interests in providing secure and effective support, or compliance with a legal obligation. Providing support information is voluntary, but a working contact address and enough detail to understand the issue may be necessary to provide a useful response.

8. Recipients and service providers

Support correspondence may be processed by email, hosting or technical service providers acting for byBartonek, only as needed to deliver those services. Application files placed in a user-selected third-party folder are processed by that storage provider under the user's relationship with the provider. byBartonek does not sell personal data.

9. Retention

  • drill files and exports remain in the user-selected location until the user changes or deletes them;
  • local settings remain until they are cleared or the application is uninstalled;
  • support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request, security, legal obligations or defensible product records.

10. Children

JB_Drill is a tool for coaches and other adult users. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering unnecessary identifying information about athletes, especially minors, into labels or notes.

11. Your choices and rights

Because application content is stored in locations you control, you can access, copy, edit or delete it directly. For personal data contained in support correspondence, or for any other privacy request, contact privacy@bybartonek.com. Applicable legal rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Czech supervisory authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection, or with another competent supervisory authority.

12. Automated decisions

byBartonek does not use personal data processed through JB_Drill support for automated decision-making or profiling.

13. Security

JB_Drill limits access to the storage location selected by the user. No method of electronic storage is completely secure; users remain responsible for device security, backups and access controls for their chosen storage provider.

14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when JB_Drill features or distribution methods change. The current version and effective date will always be published on this page.
