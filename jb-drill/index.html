THE IDEA

Finally, software that helps.

Great coaching ideas shouldn’t get stuck in software. JB_Drill is built for coaches who create their own drills. They can sketch the action almost as naturally as they would on a board. The app handles the repetitive details, brings the action to life, and makes the result easy to share with players before practice. Less time explaining at the board. More time coaching on the ice.

Paper is fast for a sketch. JB_Drill stays fast all the way to a finished drill.

JB_Drill is built for touch on Android tablets and comfortable mouse-and-keyboard editing on Windows PCs. The same drill format works on both.

SMART DRAWING

The rink understands context.

Draw first—there is no need to choose a line type before every stroke. JB_Drill infers skating, passes, shots and other actions from context, removing most of the tool switching that slows drill drawing down in conventional editors. Routes connect to players, passes and other routes while following puck possession. JB_Drill can also add matching techniques and recognize when a player collects a puck from a pile. Reshape the play and those connections stay with it.

Shape the movement ▶ Adjust the action right on the rink.

ANIMATE

Make timing visible.

JB_Drill builds an initial schedule from the event graph, queues and player links. Scrub the timeline, move a player along an existing route, or adjust the speed and wait time of one section. Global speed, symmetric second phases and queue rotation remain editable without redrawing the drill.

Sketch the drill ▶ Draw the action as naturally as you would on a board.

TACTICS

Animate formations, not just routes.

Link players into a unit, place formation keyframes and move either an individual or the entire shape. Formation overlays, player labels, puck movement and independent timing make the same canvas useful for breakouts, special teams, defensive structure and lineup presentations.

Show the tactics ▶ Make formation movement clear before players take the ice.

JB_PLAY

One link. The whole session.

Share the complete session with one click. The link opens JB_Play directly in a browser, so the recipient needs no JB_Drill installation or account. JB_Play is an interactive online player: browse every drill in the session, play or scrub the animation, and open the notes and equipment list whenever needed.

Share the movement ▶ Nothing to install—just open, browse, play, scrub and read.

LIBRARY, SESSIONS & EXPORTS

From library to locker room.

Save every drill to a searchable Library, then combine proven ideas into a complete training session with notes, tags and automatic equipment counts. When the plan is ready, export it as a polished multipage PDF or a continuous MP4 carrying the club's own identity.

Build once. Share your way. ▶ Reuse saved drills, assemble the session, then export a complete PDF or MP4.

PAINT

Sometimes freehand says it best.

Sketch a drill the old-school way, just as you would on paper. Add notes and freehand ideas over an existing drill, erase individual strokes or clear the entire painting. Paint is tuned for mouse and touch, with pressure-sensitive pen support for natural line weight.

Draw it old school ▶ Sketch with a mouse, finger or pressure-sensitive pen—and erase only what you mean to.

CONCEPT A / FEATURE STORIES

Show fewer things. Make each one memorable.

Four chapters group related features around the moment in which they help. Large demonstrations carry the page instead of turning every detail into an equal card.

01 / USE THE WHOLE RINK
The rink completes the pass.

Aim through the boards and JB_Drill solves the bounce. Follow the perimeter and the pass becomes a rim pass. Training equipment can align to the boards as naturally as the puck moves around them.

  • Bank Pass
  • Rim Pass
  • Board alignment
Let the rink finish the geometry ▶ Bank passes, rim passes and board-aware placement belong together.
02 / READ THE ACTION
Draw the event. Get the right notation.

A pass crossing an obstacle becomes a saucer pass. A route over a stick receives a jump marker. Cross a loose puck or a pile and the player picks it up, changing the route to puck carrying automatically.

  • Automatic Saucer Pass
  • Automatic stick jump
  • Automatic puck pickup
Context becomes notation ▶ The diagram explains what happened without extra setup.
03 / ADD THE SKILL
A technique changes the route.

Drop Pivot, Transition or a Finesse technique onto an existing curve. The route adapts around it. Supported obstacles can place a deke automatically, a Toe Drag Release creates and aims its shot, and a qualifying trajectory generates its own Punch Turn.

  • Technique drag & drop
  • Automatic Finesse
  • Toe Drag Release auto aim
  • Generated Punch Turn
Skills that understand the route ▶ Technique marks are part of the event, not decoration on top.
04 / REUSE AND PRESENT
Do the setup once.

Clone the first queued player and the following slots fill themselves. Generate the opposite phase with symmetry. When the plan is ready, Drill Notes and club identity become part of a polished PDF instead of a second document to assemble.

  • Automatic Queues
  • Symmetry
  • Drill Notes
  • Export identity
  • Branded PDF
From setup to finished handout ▶ Automation continues after the last route is drawn.
