JB_Drill is now available for Android tablets and Windows PCs.

This is the first public release of a tool built around a simple idea: creating a hockey drill should feel as natural as drawing it on a coaching board — while still giving you the advantages of animation, reusable content and clean digital sharing.

> Session planning is finally a joy.

## See it in action

[Watch JB_Drill in action](https://youtu.be/7rdUVfWZegA?si=kw8NAFkDHmj0s1Gu)

## Built for the way coaches think

A drill rarely begins as a finished diagram. It starts with a line, a player, a puck and an idea of what should happen next.

JB_Drill keeps that process fluid. Draw the action first, then add timing, tactical detail and presentation only where they help.

## From first sketch to shared play

- **Draw naturally.** Smart Drawing responds to the rink, players, puck possession and nearby objects.
- **Make timing visible.** Turn the drill into an animated sequence with player movement, passes and cues.
- **Explain the detail.** Use tactical keyframes, labels and notes to make decisions clear.
- **Build your library.** Save, search and reuse drills instead of drawing the same things again.
- **Share the result.** Export to PDF or MP4, or let players explore the session through [JB_Play](https://play.bybartonek.com/).

## One format. Two platforms.


| Android tablets | Windows PCs |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Touch-first drawing and editing | Comfortable mouse-and-keyboard workflow |
| Designed for work directly at the rink | Designed for detailed preparation at the desk |
| Available through Google Play | Available through Microsoft Store |


The same drill format works on both platforms, so you can start on one device and continue on the other.
