The included library grows with ten new drills. Each one is a starting point rather than a locked illustration: open it, move players, change the timing and save the result as your own drill.

## Built to be changed

The new examples cover puck movement, player timing and small-area decisions. They can be added directly to a session and exported alongside your own material.
