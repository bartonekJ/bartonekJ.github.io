# Store symbols

- Apple glyph: Simple Icons, CC0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/apple.svg . Replaces the App Store glyph at the user's request.
- Google Play glyph: Simple Icons, CC0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/googleplay.svg . This is the black silhouette from the user-approved preview.
- Microsoft Store glyph: Microsoft Fluent UI System Icons, MIT, https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-system-icons/blob/main/assets/Store%20Microsoft/SVG/ic_fluent_store_microsoft_24_filled.svg . Licence retained in LICENSE-microsoft.txt.

These are decorative store identifiers beside the explicit store name. They are not newly generated download/pre-order badges, and no brand endorsement or trademark licence is implied by an open-source artwork licence. App Store remains Coming soon until public release.
